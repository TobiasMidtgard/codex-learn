The whole idea
Vout = 5 V · R2 / (R1+R2)
Two resistors in series carry the same current. So the supply voltage splits in proportion to resistance — nothing more mysterious than that.
Where it bites
The formula only holds while nothing draws current from the Vout node. Attach a load and the ratio shifts — you'll prove that two steps from now.
